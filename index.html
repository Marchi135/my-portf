About Me

I didn't start in tech. I started with people — and that made all the difference.

8+ years in office administration and customer service taught me how businesses really work: the gaps, the friction, and the moments where the right tool would change everything. Today, I build those tools.

My career began in administrative and client-facing roles — managing operations, documentation, and communications. Those years gave me something most developers don't have: a deep understanding of how non-technical people experience technology.

I kept noticing the same pattern — manual processes, inefficient systems, tools that created more work than they solved. Instead of working around those problems, I got curious. That curiosity led me to code.

I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then expanded into React, Angular, Bootstrap, and Tailwind. I added IT support skills so I understand both how systems are built and how real people interact with them.

"Good systems should support people, not overwhelm them. That belief shapes everything I build."

Not starting over — building forward. Every solution I create is designed for real people, especially non-technical users.
